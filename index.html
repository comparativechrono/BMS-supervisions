Before you start

  • Use a laptop or desktop computer with an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. A wide screen helps, because the instructions sit beside the workbench. It also works on a tablet or a phone, although the screen is cramped.
  • Everything you do – your essays, ticks, answers and files – is saved in this browser, on this computer, and every supervision’s page shares your files. Use another computer, or a private window, and you start again.
  • Some lab and library computers clear the browser when you log out, so keep a copy of your work: My files downloads everything, and each essay downloads as a Word document.

How the supervisions work

  • The work to hand in is in the first tab, Supervision work. Write it as in the exam: typed, in one go, with no notes and no pasting from elsewhere. Then submit it and hand it in, from the same page, with the hand-in password that Tim Hearn gives you.
  • The instructions on the left take you through the week’s exercises. Their steps tick themselves as you do them, and every question has a model answer you can show once you have had a go.
  • Parts marked If you have time are extras. The other questions in Supervision work are for practice and revision: only the essay is to be handed in.
